# v16: bounded primary-source navigation packets

The bottleneck is manual orchestration of primary-source research, not the
already optimized local planner. Persistent authorization caches risk stale
receipts; concurrent sending risks duplicates. Choose bounded research URL
bundles instead: twenty fresh preflight survivors, at most four source hints per
navigation group, and shared URLs linked to all candidate indexes.

The planner performs no network actions. Website/domain hints and cached sources
remain unverified; missing or unsafe hints stay unresolved. Official product and
role evidence, public contact, daily agency read, canonical ICP >70, live CRM and
mailbox dedup remain mandatory. Ready customers do not wait for a complete batch.
Sending is serial with receipt persistence before the next send.

Tests cover URL dedup, bounded groups, unsafe hints, missing websites, packet
boundaries and preserved live checks. Measure navigation grouping, not promised
end-to-end speed or reply rate. Preserve private Site audience when publishing.
